# Context handoff — review before importing

## Source and target

Source chat/project, date/revision, intended target and purpose of the transfer.

## Approved decisions

Decision, reason, who approved it and source reference. State whether it still applies to the target.

## Observed implementation

Facts with source path/symbol/revision and actual verification evidence. Keep planned behavior separate.

## Domain terms and constraints

Terms, product invariants, architectural constraints and their original scope. Identify source-specific names or paths to adapt.

## Open work and uncertainties

Outstanding tasks, tentative ideas, conflicts and unanswered questions. Include failures and unavailable checks.

## Existing conventions to preserve

AGENTS/CLAUDE/README pointers, glossary, package manager, test commands, tracker, host/provider bindings and approved scope.

## Proposed transfer

What to bring, what to leave behind, proposed destinations and decisions still needed from the user. Exclude credentials and unrelated private information.
